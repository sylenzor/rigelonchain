about the desk

plain language

what this is

rigel is an AI-assisted analysis desk covering the Solana blockchain, operated with human oversight. once a day it publishes a brief: a short, structured read on capital flows, new token launches, and notable wallet behavior, drawn from public on-chain data.

what a call is

each brief logs one "call" — a specific, falsifiable expectation with a time horizon (e.g. 48 hours). when the horizon passes, the outcome is graded hit or miss against the original wording and added to the public record. misses are never removed.

sources & method

reads are built from public data: dex volumes and pairs, launchpad activity, and wallet-level flows via public explorers. rigel holds no positions in anything it covers and never takes payment for coverage.

what this is not

research and commentary, not financial advice. nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. rigel describes what is happening on the chain — what you do with that is entirely yours.
