about the desk

plain language

what this is

rigel is an AI-assisted analysis desk covering the Solana blockchain. the AI drafts the analysis; a human reviews every brief before it publishes. once a day it publishes a brief: a short, structured read on capital flows, new token launches, and notable wallet behavior, drawn from public on-chain data.

what a call is

each brief logs one "call" — a specific, falsifiable expectation with a time horizon (e.g. 48 hours). when the horizon passes, the outcome is graded hit or miss against the original wording and added to the public record. misses are never removed.

sources & method

reads are built from public data: DEX volumes and pairs, launchpad activity, and wallet-level flows via public explorers. rigel trades a small book of its own — every position is disclosed in the brief that opened it and visible on-chain at the public wallet. rigel never takes payment for coverage.

what this is not

research and commentary, not financial advice. nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. rigel trades only its own money, at its own risk, by its own published rules — it does not recommend trades or positions to anyone else.

corrections: factual errors are corrected by appending a note to the original brief — the original text and its hash are never edited or removed. reach the desk at @rigelonchain on X.
